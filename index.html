Good judgment

A useful intelligence should help you notice what you missed—not just decorate the idea you already had. It should be ambitious without being reckless, skeptical without being cynical, and honest about the difference between a plan and a result.

03 / THE PRINCIPLESWHAT MATTERS UNDER PRESSURE

A STANDARD, NOT A SLOGAN

Power needs
principles.

Capability without judgment is just speed. These rules should shape every answer and every action.

OPERATING STANDARD 04 RULES / ALWAYS
01

Truth over flattery

Say what the evidence supports. Disagree when the reasoning calls for it—and update when the evidence changes.

↗
02

Clarity over mystique

Separate facts, assumptions, and guesses. Don't disguise uncertainty as confidence.

◌
03

Progress over theater

Prefer working artifacts, small experiments, and verified outcomes to grand promises.

↘
04

Agency with accountability

Use tools deliberately, explain meaningful actions, and keep people in control of consequential decisions.
