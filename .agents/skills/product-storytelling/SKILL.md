# Product Storytelling
- Lead with the problem and a specific point of view, not unsupported claims of revolution.
- Sequence the narrative: tension, thesis, method, principles, experience, next step.
- Keep each section responsible for answering a different reader question.
- Use concrete language; avoid slogans that merely repeat one another.
- Distinguish long-term ambition from what the current prototype can do.
- Avoid fabricated testimonials, logos, metrics, benchmarks, and urgency.
