# Creative Direction
- Pick a target feeling, one visual thesis, and a small set of principles before styling.
- Avoid generic AI gradients, interchangeable SaaS cards, and decoration without meaning.
- Use contrast, composition, typography, scale, negative space, and rhythm as the primary design tools.
- Use accent color as a signal for action or emphasis, not as a universal fill.
- Make the hero composition memorable and carry its language consistently throughout.
- Evaluate the design at desktop, tablet, and narrow mobile widths.
- Keep the site legible when motion or external fonts are unavailable.
