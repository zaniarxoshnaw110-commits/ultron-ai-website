# ULTRON Design System — Concept 004

## Visual thesis
A precise, editorial intelligence instrument. The site should feel considered and kinetic, not like a generic AI landing page or an imitation of a fictional interface.

## Palette
- Canvas: #0b0d0b
- Surface: #121511 / #181c16
- Primary text: #f2f3e9
- Secondary text: #a2a69b
- Signal lime: #d5ff52
- Warm contrast: #ff795e
- Dividers: translucent warm neutral
- Fonts: Space Grotesk display, Manrope body, DM Mono technical labels

## Layout
- Split editorial hero with a procedural particle field.
- Asymmetric thesis layout, four-step operating model, principles list, and functional prototype console.
- Accent lime is reserved for active state, meaningful emphasis, and primary action.
- Mobile layout reflows into a vertical narrative; flow cards and scenario selectors become compact grids.
- Metadata should support hierarchy rather than overwhelm the content.

## Motion and interaction
- The particle field responds subtly to pointer movement and pauses when the page is hidden.
- Honor reduced-motion preferences and cap canvas device pixel ratio.
- Navigation state is exposed through ARIA attributes and Escape closes the menu.
- Scenario selection updates the sample response and selected state.
- Custom prompts stay local; keyword matching selects a preset and explicitly discloses the limitation.
